# Footer pages: content and publication review

The five new pages use src/pages/support.jsx, src/support-content.json and src/support-pages.css. The layout reuses the About navigation and footer. Edit copy in support-content.json; metadata remains in each root HTML entry. Run npm run build after edits.

These are local policy drafts, not a certification of legal compliance. Before public deployment, have the operator and an appropriately qualified adviser confirm:
- Legal operator name, registered address, governing jurisdiction, effective date and complaint/grievance contact.
- Actual verification and moderation practices, organizer eligibility, prohibited campaigns, foreign contribution restrictions where applicable, payout checks and timing.
- Refund eligibility, request deadlines, handling of disbursed funds, charges and expected processing times. The site provides a support request path; this change does not implement automated refunds.
- Privacy data inventory, purposes and legal bases, recipients/processors, hosting locations/transfers, retention periods, deletion process, children's data handling, security measures and applicable rights/complaint procedures.
- The existing support email and phone actually belong to the operator and are monitored. Existing footer contact details were not independently verified.
- Whether legal terms must be explicitly accepted during registration or campaign creation; these pages do not add a consent flow.

No fixed refund window, guaranteed verification, guaranteed payout, tax deduction or full compliance claim has been invented. Replace the general review language with approved operational rules before publishing. Add an effective date only when adopted.

Sources consulted:

- Government of India, MeitY, Digital Personal Data Protection Rules 2025: https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies
The applicable rules and commencement dates require a business-specific review; these drafts do not assert that every provision is already in force.
